On Mac, `Cmd+Shift+4` lets you take a screenshot of a selected area. Instead of saving the file, a thumbnail preview pops up in the bottom-right corner.

You can drag that thumbnail directly into the Claude CLI to attach the image to your prompt.

## Steps

1. Press `Cmd+Shift+4` and drag to select the area you want to capture
2. Wait for the thumbnail preview to appear in the bottom-right corner
3. Drag the thumbnail into the Claude CLI terminal window

That's it — the image is attached and Claude can see it.

## Tips

- The thumbnail disappears after a few seconds, so drag it quickly
- `Cmd+Shift+4` then `Space` lets you screenshot a specific window instead of a selection
- `Cmd+Shift+3` takes a full-screen screenshot (also draggable)
